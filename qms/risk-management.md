# QMS Best Practices: Risk Management

## Options

### Option A: ISO 14971-style risk process (Recommended for regulated)
- **Description**: Identify, evaluate, mitigate risks; track residual risk; review periodically.
- **Pros**: Systematic, audit-ready.
- **Cons**: Heavy for small projects.
- **Best for**: Medical/safety-critical.

### Option B: Lightweight risk register
- **Description**: Simple risk list with likelihood/impact and mitigations.
- **Pros**: Low overhead.
- **Cons**: Less formal.
- **Best for**: Software products.

## Recommendation
Option B for software; Option A for regulated/safety-critical.

## Rules
- Maintain a risk register.
- Review risks each release.
- Link mitigations to requirements/tests.
- Reassess on changes.
- Document accepted residual risks with rationale.
